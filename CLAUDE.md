# Project notes

## Player-facing texts

Every text a player reads (info popups, hovers, hints, toasts, room choices, descriptions) follows one style:

- Plain, full sentences. No dash asides (" - "), no brackets, no "X: Y" shorthand.
- Say only what a player needs. Leave out what is learned by playing (timers, draw mechanics, payback, provably fair details).
- Short: one or two sentences per text.
- No hovers that explain single words inside card texts.

Examples:

- "Use the map to navigate further. Rooms can be sensed scaling with the instinct attribute."
- "The house keeps 5% of the pot, but never more than half of the other players' coins. You never win less than you bet."
- "With luck you find gear. If it fails, you take 25% damage."
